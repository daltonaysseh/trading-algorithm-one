How this works

Every analyzed call is read in full and scored 0–100 on five independently-judged communication dimensions. The composite score is just their mean — it isn't a separate, independently-judged number. This page explains what each dimension is actually looking for, why it's relevant to equity research, and what it looked like in three real calls.

What this IS

A read of how confident and transparent management sounded on one specific call — the actual dimension scores, tied to real quotes from the transcript.

What this ISN'T

A stock price predictor, a buy/sell signal, a substitute for reading or listening to the actual call, or investment advice of any kind.
