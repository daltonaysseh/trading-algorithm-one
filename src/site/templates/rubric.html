The scoring rubric

Every transcript is scored 0–100 on 5 independently-judged dimensions. The overall "composite" score is just their average — it isn't a separate, independently-judged number.

This rubric hasn't been independently audited at scale. It's adapted from an earlier project that had no written scoring criteria at all, and its anchors were sharpened once after real testing showed two dimensions weren't discriminating well (see Limitations). Treat every score as a structured first read of communication style — not a validated, audited signal, and not a prediction of anything.
